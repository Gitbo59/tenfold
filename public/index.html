      Ranked match

      You'll be matched with someone near your rating. Win to climb. Beating a higher-rated player earns more.

      How ranked works
      • Every match uses the same rules: 3 lives, 20 seconds an answer, no hints, on one shared board.
      • Score more than your opponent to win. If they run out of lives while you're ahead, the match ends there.
      • Resigning, or being disconnected for over a minute, is a loss. A match that never starts changes nothing.
      • It's an Elo-style rating: everyone starts at 1200, and new players move faster for their first 20 games. We run automatic fair-play checks. Read the rules.
